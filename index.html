About

I'm an AI engineer with four years of experience building agent systems and the backend services they depend on. Most of my work sits at the point where a model stops being a demo and has to survive real traffic: orchestration, isolation, evaluation, and the failure modes that only show up at concurrency.

At Testing Mavens I architected a VS Code–integrated agentic platform for project-aware development and testing. It centres on a Learning Agent that continuously analyses project artifacts to evolve an application knowledge base, which downstream testing agents read from to generate and execute end-to-end browser tests on their own. I built the orchestration layer on Kubernetes — isolated sandboxes, resource controls, runtime monitoring — supporting hundreds of concurrent agents in production, and an LLM-as-a-judge pipeline to measure whether their output is actually correct.

Before that, at Innovature Labs, I built RAG pipelines over 10,000+ enterprise documents across Milvus, Chroma, and Azure AI Search, multi-step LangGraph workflows with human-in-the-loop validation of plans and tool calls, and the high-throughput REST and WebSocket APIs that served them. I also modelled a Neo4j knowledge graph behind a recommendation engine and cut release cycles in half with GitHub Actions and automated testing.

I care most about the unglamorous half of AI engineering — tracing, evaluation, sandboxing, cost, and knowing why a run failed. I also write and record explanations of infrastructure concepts, mostly because teaching something is the fastest way to find the gaps in your own understanding.

Open to conversations about agent infrastructure, evaluation systems, and backend platform work. Send me an email.
